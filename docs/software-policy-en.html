Software and source use policy

VietLicenSure is provided free of charge but is not released under an open-source licence. This page explains the change introduced in v4.9 and the current terms in SOURCE-POLICY-v5.0.

1. Status and controlling source

Current policy: SOURCE-POLICY-v5.0, updated 24 September 2026. docs/archive/v4.x/SOURCE-POLICY-v4.9.md is retained only as a historical record of the transition to a controlled-source model.

If introductory material differs from the policy document, the current repository policy controls. Each release identity must still be verified using the manifest, checksums and signature bundled with that exact package.

2. Rights to use the official executable

Permitted

  • Download and use the unmodified official executable free of charge.
  • Use it lawfully for personal, educational, research, community and internal organisational purposes.
  • Share the official download link so others can download and verify it themselves.
  • Inspect the signature, SHA-256, manifest and provenance information.
  • Submit defects, proposals, documentation, translations and feedback through official channels.

Written approval required

  • Rehosting, mirroring or distributing files from another server or repository.
  • Bundling with another product, selling, renting, charging for or commercialising it.
  • Modifying, patching or reverse engineering to reuse code or create derivatives.
  • Bypassing official-build verification, rebranding or impersonating the author.
  • Removing or altering copyright, Build ID, signature, hashes or official-source information.

3. Controlled source since v4.9

Implementation source from v4.9 onward is not licensed under GPL, MIT, Apache or an equivalent open-source licence. Technical visibility or download capability does not itself grant permission to copy, modify, publish, integrate, create derivative works from or commercialise the source.

Controlled scope includes user-interface and business logic, detection/remediation rules, modules and technical data, build/sign/package/update components, internal test assets and unpublished technical documentation.

4. Requesting source access

A request to study, research, review security or contribute at source level must be sent to thanhvietit.hopnghia@gmail.com and identify:

  1. Name, organisation and contact details.
  2. Specific purpose, expected outcome and requested source scope.
  3. Requested duration, approved persons, environment and safeguards.
  4. Any copying, build, modification or validation requirement.
  5. Agreement to the policy and additional conditions.

Access exists only after written approval. It is personal, non-transferable, limited by purpose/scope/duration and may be suspended or revoked when a violation or risk is found.

5. Limits on approved access

  • Do not share accounts or expose source to unapproved persons.
  • Do not copy source outside the approved environment, publish, mirror or upload it elsewhere.
  • Do not bundle, integrate, create derivatives, serve third parties or commercialise it.
  • Do not use source as training data or input to code-generation systems without approval.
  • Do not remove attribution, rebrand, impersonate or prematurely disclose vulnerabilities.

6. Community contributions

Source access is not required to contribute defect reports, reproduction steps, feature proposals, documentation, translations, test cases, compatibility results or sanitised sample data.

Source-level contributions require written terms covering scope, branch/channel, quality gates, attribution, copyright and confidentiality. Sending an idea or patch does not transfer ownership automatically and does not grant rights to the remaining source.

8. Security and sensitive data

Do not publish product keys, tokens, credentials, personal/internal data, controlled source, sensitive reports or uncoordinated vulnerabilities. Submit vulnerabilities through GitHub Private Security Advisory or the official email channel.

9. Verifying an official build

Download only from the official repository/release and verify the version, Build ID, SHA-256, manifest, checksums, author and signature state. The current channel is Official Self-Signed: it uses the official pinned self-issued signer and a timestamp, but is not public-CA, EV or Microsoft Store signing.
