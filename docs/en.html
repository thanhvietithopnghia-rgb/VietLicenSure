Interpret results correctly

“Activated” does not prove legal entitlement

VietLicenSure separates assessment layers so a technical signal is not presented as a legal conclusion.

Technical statusActivation, licensing state, signatures, and services.
Entitlement evidenceInvoices, agreements, accounts, or licensing portals.
Tampering indicatorsAn active mechanism versus a residual file only.
Security postureDefender, Secure Boot, BitLocker, and time health.
Scope: technical results support assessment and administration. Ownership still requires purchase or organizational licensing records.

Get started
